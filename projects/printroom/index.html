Printroom

A fast, Photos-style home for your Lightroom cloud library and iCloud Photos. Browse, filter and map your photos, relive trips as Journeys, and keep the ones you love available offline.

Journeys groups located photos into trips away from home and draws the route on the map, with flight arcs, ground paths and photo tiles you can scrub through and relive memories. Use the Smart Map that follows your filters to find that exact photo you remember. Custom Offline rules so that you can keep your most important photos right at your fingertips without bloating up your storage needs or manually labeling photos. Lightning Fast: The app opens up instantly and allows you to seamlessly scrub though your photos, so that every flick is a story.

SupportPrivacy policy

Currently in beta testing on iPhone, iPad and Mac. Lightroom features require an Adobe account with a paid Lightroom plan.

Map and Journeys

See where your photos were taken, with your filters applied. Journeys find your trips and replay them stop by stop along the route.

Search and People

Search places, keywords, cameras and people. Optional visual search finds photos by what's in them, entirely on your device.

Offline rules

Choose photos with smart-collection-style rules and keep high-resolution copies on your device for viewing without a connection.

Your library at your fingertips

Scroll tens of thousands of photos in a fast seamless grid, jump by year and month, and zoom into any shot.

Lightroom and iCloud Photos

Connect your Lightroom cloud catalog, turn on iCloud Photos, or use both.

Edits that sync back

Set star ratings, flags, color labels and keywords, and organize albums. Lightroom changes sync back to your catalog.

Advanced Filtering

Narrow by rating, flag, color label, camera, lens, exposure, date, place, people or source, while also filtering by map region

Private by design

There is no Printroom server or account. Your photos stay between your device, Adobe and Apple.

How it works

Sign in with your Adobe account inside the app. Printroom uses Adobe's secure sign-in to access your Lightroom catalog, then downloads photo previews and metadata directly from Adobe to your device. Ratings, flags, labels, keywords and album changes you make go straight back to Adobe. iCloud Photos is read on your device with your permission. There is no Printroom server and no Printroom account.
