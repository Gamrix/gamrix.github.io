Printroom

A Lightroom viewer for iPhone and Mac. Browse, filter and map your Lightroom cloud photos with the ease of Apple Photos, and keep the ones you love available offline.

Support Privacy policy

Currently in private testing. Requires an Adobe account with a Lightroom plan.

Your Lightroom library, Photos-style

Browse every photo in your Lightroom cloud catalog in a fast, familiar grid modeled on Apple Photos.

Automatic sync

Printroom reads your catalog, collections and photo metadata through Adobe's Lightroom API and keeps it current.

Filters that match Lightroom

Narrow by star rating, flag, color label, camera, date and more, the same way Lightroom's filters work.

Map view

See where your photos were taken. The map and filters work together, so you can map only your best shots.

Offline rules

Choose photos with smart-collection-style rules and keep high-resolution copies on your device for viewing without a connection.

Read-only and private

Printroom never changes your Lightroom catalog. Your photos stay between your device and Adobe.

How it works

Sign in with your Adobe account inside the app. Printroom uses Adobe's secure sign-in to get read-only access to your Lightroom catalog, then downloads photo previews and metadata directly from Adobe to your device. There is no Printroom server and no Printroom account.
