Future Turtles Designs

Signs, menus, stickers and patterns made for Future Turtles, a camp in Black Rock City. Most were drawn white on black for laser etching and cut in acrylic.

Free to use and rework non-commercially under CC BY-NC 4.0. Lettering uses Iconian's OmegaForce font.
